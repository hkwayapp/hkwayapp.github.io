私隱政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective date: 2026年10月8日

我們收集的資料 · Data We Collect

本 App 不要求建立帳戶，亦不會要求你的姓名、電郵地址或付款卡資料。本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能會處理裝置識別資料、由 IP 位址推算的大概位置、廣告資料、App 使用互動資料、效能及診斷資料，以提供、量度及改善廣告服務。

This app does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, or payment-card details. The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising data, app interactions, performance data, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

廣告選擇 · Advertising Choices

在適用地區，本 App 會在要求廣告前顯示私隱及廣告同意選項。即使用戶拒絕個人化廣告同意，仍可繼續使用遊戲。用戶亦可透過非消耗型 App 內購買永久移除廣告。

Where required, the app presents privacy and advertising consent choices before requesting ads. The game remains usable if the user declines personalized advertising consent. Users may also permanently remove advertisements through a non-consumable in-app purchase.

裝置上的資料 · Data Stored on Your Device

遊戲進度、遊戲統計、語言及外觀設定只會儲存在你的裝置上，用於提供 App 功能。你可以在 App 內重設遊戲進度，亦可以刪除 App 以移除其本機資料。

Game progress, gameplay statistics, language, and appearance preferences are stored only on your device to provide app functionality. You can reset game progress inside the app or delete the app to remove its local data.

資料保留及控制 · Data Retention and Control

儲存在裝置上的資料會保留至你在 App 內重設相關資料或刪除 App 為止。

Data stored on your device remains until you reset the relevant data in the app or delete the app.

本 App 並非專為兒童而設。廣告服務所處理的資料受上述廣告同意選項及 Google 的私隱政策規管。

The app is not specifically directed to children. Data processed by the advertising service is governed by the consent choices described above and Google’s privacy policy.
